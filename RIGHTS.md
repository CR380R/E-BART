# Rights and Responsible Reuse

This dataset accompanies *A Neural Model to Jointly Predict and Explain Truthfulness of Statements* (Brand et al., *Journal of Data and Information Quality*, 15(1), Article 4, 2023; DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/3546917).

## Model Code

The six model files in `Source Code/` are unchanged copies from the public `CR380R/E-BART` repository at commit `a8e8438d1b8ed68ddb0d5c7dfbe3df4206d3cc03`. Erik Brand is the original author. The curator reports permission to include these files in this research release. The original repository does not contain a software license. Permission to include the code here does not grant a general license for other uses.

## Crowd Data

The release contains the study's pseudonymous crowd judgments, written justifications, task-level comments, completion and quality-control records, selected MTurk assignment metadata, and browser/device categories. These research records are provided to support reproducibility. Participant contributions do not automatically fall under any license applying to author-created material.

Original participant and platform identifiers, MTurk HIT and assignment IDs, precise geolocation, IP addresses, raw user-agent strings, access tokens, and the reversible worker mapping are not included. Pseudonymization does not guarantee anonymity: open-ended responses, timestamps, and combinations of task or technical fields may still be linkable. Do not attempt to identify or contact participants, or connect these records to external personal information. Users should respect applicable privacy obligations when analyzing or redistributing the data.

## Claims and explanations

The statements and reference labels in `Ground_Truth.csv` come from FEVER research data. The associated e-FEVER and E-BART experimental explanations are separately retained in `Explanations.csv`. FEVER includes material from Wikipedia. Follow the original FEVER dataset's [license terms](https://fever.ai/download/fever/license.html), which reference the applicable Wikipedia article licenses and, in their absence, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Cite **James Thorne, Andreas Vlachos, Christos Christodoulopoulos, and Arpit Mittal (2018), *FEVER: a Large-scale Dataset for Fact Extraction and VERification*** and follow the applicable attribution and reuse conditions.

The `reference_explanation` e-FEVER summaries and `ebart_explanation` outputs generated by E-BART in `Explanations.csv` are preserved from the materials used for the human evaluation. These explanations come from different sources than the FEVER statements. The FEVER license does **not** automatically apply to all of them. The **70 selected e-FEVER reference summaries** are included to document the human evaluation. Their inclusion does not grant rights to redistribute the complete e-FEVER corpus or override the rights of the original source holders. Consult the original e-FEVER research and relevant rights holders for additional reuse obligations.

The task configurations contain study statements and explanation material. Original worker lists, access tokens and internal service endpoints are not included.

No single license is provided for the whole release.
